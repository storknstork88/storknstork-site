Delete your Tinker Survival IO account

Tinker Survival IO, developed by StorknStork

You can delete your Tinker Survival IO account and its associated data at any time, either inside the Game or by email. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete in the Game (immediate)

  1. Open Tinker Survival IO and go to the main menu.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. If your account is linked to Google, sign in with Google again when asked.

Your account is deleted immediately and the Game restarts as a new guest.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the Game installed or cannot open it, send an email to support@storknstork.com with:

  • the subject "Account deletion request";
  • your Player ID (shown in the Game under Settings), or the Google account email you linked to the Game.

We may ask you to confirm that you own the account. We process requests within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account and sign-in record (guest or linked Google sign-in);
  • all game progress: stages, items, currencies, upgrades, missions and run results;
  • your transaction and reward records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • anti-fraud records of rewarded-ad transactions that do not contain your Player ID — deleted after 30 days;
  • server logs — deleted under our providers' standard retention periods;
  • encrypted database backups — overwritten within 7 days.

See our Privacy Policy for details.
