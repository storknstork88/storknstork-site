Privacy Policy — Tinker Survival IO

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StorknStork ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you play Tinker Survival IO (the "Game") on Android. If you have questions, contact us at support@storknstork.com.

1. Information we collect

Account and game data (stored on our servers). When you first open the Game, an anonymous guest account is created for you automatically. We store:

  • a random account identifier (your Player ID);
  • your game progress, such as stages cleared, items, currencies, upgrades, missions and run results;
  • records of in-game transactions and rewarded-ad rewards, used to prevent duplicates and cheating.

Google account (optional). If you choose to link a Google account to protect your progress, we receive a Google account identifier and the email address and display name associated with it through Firebase Authentication. We use this only to sign you in and recover your progress. Linking is optional; you can play as a guest.

Advertising data. The Game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Our ad provider, Google AdMob, may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to serve, measure and (where you have consented) personalize ads.

Technical and diagnostic data. Our backend providers automatically record limited technical data such as IP address, request time and error details in server logs. If crash reporting is enabled in your version of the Game, crash and error reports (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces) are sent to Firebase Crashlytics.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The Game has no in-app purchases, so we do not process payment information.

2. How we use information

  • to run the Game, save your progress on our servers and restore it on another device;
  • to grant rewards (including rewards for watched ads) exactly once and to detect cheating or abuse;
  • to show rewarded ads and to respect your consent choices;
  • to fix bugs, keep the service secure and respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics) — hosting of accounts and game data. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google AdMob — rewarded advertising. How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — collecting your advertising consent where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland).

Our servers are operated by Google Cloud in the asia-southeast1 (Singapore) region, so your data may be processed outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Your advertising choices

Where required, the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings → Privacy options (shown when it applies to your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the Game: Settings → Delete account, type DELETE to confirm (accounts linked to Google must sign in with Google again). Deletion happens immediately.
  • On the web: follow the instructions at storknstork.com/tinker-survival-io/delete-account.html.

Deletion removes your account, your sign-in record, your game progress, your transaction and reward records and your pending run results. Limited data is kept only as follows: anti-fraud records of rewarded-ad transactions that do not contain your Player ID are deleted after 30 days; server logs are deleted under our providers' standard retention periods; and encrypted database backups are overwritten within 7 days.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections and stored by Google Cloud with access restricted to the developer. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@storknstork.com and include your Player ID (Settings in the Game). We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
